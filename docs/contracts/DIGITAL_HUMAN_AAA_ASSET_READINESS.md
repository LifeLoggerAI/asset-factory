# URAI AAA Digital Human Paid-Asset Readiness

Status: prepared/no-spend authority.

This document converts the digital-human launch requirement into concrete paid/custom asset families without authorizing purchases, provider calls, voice cloning, likeness generation, or public release.

The machine-readable authority is `model_forge/digital-human-aaa-readiness.json`.

## Why this exists

The general Model Forge inventory correctly blocks invented likenesses, but a single generic human rig is not sufficient for an AAA launch. A complete digital human requires coordinated identity, face, eyes, mouth, hair, body, hands, wardrobe, facial performance, viseme/lip performance, voice, dialogue runtime, body motion, scene lighting/camera, accessibility derivatives, variants, and receipts.

## No bare-bones rule

Promotion requires all P0 families and representative variants. One head mesh, one stock voice, one generic walk cycle, or one static avatar cannot satisfy the launch requirement.

## Spend boundary

Every paid/provider action remains blocked until:

- a specific gap is proven against current runtime authority;
- provider/account eligibility is confirmed;
- commercial/redistribution/AI-use licensing is acceptable;
- budget is approved;
- source/identity consent and rights are retained where applicable;
- exact provider/model/version is pinned;
- the provider call has an execution receipt;
- generated output remains candidate-only until QC and human approval.

## Identity boundary

No stock/preset voice may be promoted as a user's or family member's identity. No likeness may be inferred as authorized merely because photos/video exist. Voice and likeness have separate consent/rights requirements.

## Human performance

Photoreal face, lip sync, gaze, hair/cloth, and body motion require literal visual/performance review, not only automated geometry or file validation.

## Accessibility

Captions, transcript, reduced-motion equivalents, and non-audio alternatives are P0 deliverables, not post-production extras.

## Completion

This readiness layer is complete when the manifest is CI-valid and every family is either:

- backed by an accepted current runtime asset;
- backed by a licensed/custom source and promotion receipt; or
- represented by a specifically authorized provider job with QC, rights, accessibility, performance, and human-approval evidence.

Provider availability alone is never completion.
