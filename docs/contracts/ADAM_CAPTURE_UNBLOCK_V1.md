# Adam Digital Human — Capture Unblock

This is the smallest real-world capture package that closes the major remaining source gap.

## Record one session

Record three clips from the same current appearance:

1. **Head identity** — slow front / three-quarter / profile coverage plus at least 30 seconds of natural speech.
2. **Full-body motion** — locked camera, 1080p60 minimum, full body and hands/feet continuously visible, including idle, walk, turns and natural gestures.
3. **Hands + facial performance** — medium close-up, natural expressions, gaze/blinks and both sides of the hands.

Do not use beauty filters, synthetic voice, background music or AI face processing.

The full machine-readable capture sequence is `adam-capture-unblock-v1.json`.

## Why the new capture is still required

The preserved current-adult canonical source remains the primary likeness authority and now has a seven-frame derived private contact sheet covering useful profile/three-quarter, glasses, facial-hair silhouette, hairline/ear, jawline and neck-tattoo evidence.

It does **not** contain:

- a clean true-frontal neutral hero frame;
- full body;
- walking;
- hands;
- 1080p/60 full-body mocap coverage.

Those missing observations cannot be responsibly invented by Meshy, Tripo, Rodin, Runway, HeyGen or any other provider.
