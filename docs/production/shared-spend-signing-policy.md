# Shared protected spending signing policy

The canonical gateway must keep spending approval, independent final-charge reconciliation, and deployment/control verification separate. Different role labels and different HTTP tokens do not make one signing key independent.

The actual gateway validates every configured public signing record before storage access, rejects malformed or private material, requires Ed25519, and compares canonical SPKI fingerprints plus normalized declared-principal hashes across the three purposes. Key IDs and PEM whitespace cannot reset identity. Declared subjects use Unicode NFKC and case folding for this conservative separation policy; this does not establish real-world identity or custody. Within one purpose, legitimate rotation aliases remain possible. Registry size is bounded to128 per purpose. Public-key snapshots are copied and frozen before asynchronous transaction work.

Preflight/reservation requires configured approval and reconciliation signers. Reconciliation remains non-executing and does not require a live approval registry, so a genuinely independent final charge can settle an expired or revoked grant without permitting another create.

Every new protected hold records `charge_excluded_signer_spki_sha256` and `charge_excluded_signer_subject_sha256`. These server-owned public identity hashes retain the approval/verifier roles present at admission. Removing an approval/verifier key from current configuration and re-registering it as a reconciler cannot convert that old role into an independent charge issuer. A final charge must pass both the current separated policy and the hold's original exclusions before any settlement or task/charge-claim writes. Missing, duplicate, malformed or oversized historical exclusions reject and retain the full exposure.

No paid request, reserve echo, worker protocol, provider grant, signature schema or client credential changes. Shared cash/credit/concurrency accounting, permanent semantic-input claims, exact source/wire/credential/price and controls, bounded retry/runtime, nonextendible admission, independent task/charge claims, and retained unknown-outcome quarantine remain in force.

A legacy hold lacking original signer evidence stays quarantined. An authorized custodian must recover the immutable original approval/verification public-key and declared-principal receipts and make a reviewed, independently evidenced migration before that hold can settle. The gateway never guesses those identities or silently releases exposure. There are no genuine legacy account receipts or installed credentials in this source lane.
